Something on the to-do list for months, but a contractor is too expensive for small jobs, and a specialist has no time for them either? Or the problem is technical, and the average handyman gives up.

Mat takes it on. From a dripping tap to a complete bathroom or kitchen renovation, from a printer that refuses to cooperate to wifi that finally works throughout the house.

Services

I offer a range of services tailored to the most common jobs in and around the house or office.

Small jobs

Those little jobs that have been on the list for months but never get done. A dripping tap, re-caulking the bath or kitchen, skirting boards, furniture still in the box. A flatpack wardrobe won't build itself. A lamp, painting or mirror that still needs hanging, an extra socket that would be handy, a switch in a different spot, or just something that needs a fresh coat of paint.

Home & office technology

Wifi that isn't working, a laptop that keeps freezing, or a printer that refuses to print. With a solid IT background, Mat helps where most handymen give up. These days almost everything connects via wifi: the TV, thermostat, doorbell, security camera, smart lighting, tablet or game console. Mat makes sure it just works.

Larger jobs & projects

Laminate flooring or photo wallpaper in a child's bedroom, tiling a bathroom, painting, a new sink or rain shower, replacing a kitchen worktop, or building a fitted wardrobe. But also complete renovations: renovating a bathroom, installing a kitchen, or making the attic liveable. Mat plans it, comes well prepared, thinks it through with you, and gets it finished. For larger projects, always based on a quote.
