New kitchen | Heemstede

This homeowner in Heemstede wanted to get rid of her half-open U-shaped kitchen and opted for a layout with a peninsula instead. The old kitchen was stripped out first, including a half-height wall, and all the pipework was rerouted by chasing it into the floor. Because space was limited, a deeper worktop was chosen, which doubled as a bar with room for three people to sit and eat. The kitchen was supplied by Keuken Kampioen in Cruquius. Looking to have a kitchen renovated in Heemstede? Handymat Services handles kitchen renovations and installations in homes and small offices throughout Kennemerland.

New bathroom | Haarlem

The owner of this home had one clear wish: a spacious bath. The bathroom itself was very tight on space, so a large bath with a central drain was chosen. Because the shower is positioned at the end, you don't end up standing on that drain while showering — a detail that's easily overlooked. As the budget was limited, Handymat also helped find affordable tiles and bathroom furniture. Looking for a bathroom renovation or retiling in Haarlem? Handymat Services carries out bathroom renovations throughout Kennemerland.

Full second-floor renovation | Haarlem

At this property in Haarlem, the entire second floor was stripped back and fully renovated: all the internal walls were removed and the layout was redesigned from scratch. Patio renovation | Heemstede

This patio in Heemstede badly needed a refresh: a dull, weathered tiled floor and overgrown, oversized planting that made the space feel cramped. Balcony renovation | Haarlem

On this balcony in Haarlem, the supporting beams had rotted through in several places, clearly visible from the damaged wood and sagging boards. Rather than a simple repair, a more thorough approach was chosen: the balcony was extended by 50 cm, creating genuine room for a seating area instead of a narrow walkway. The rotten structure was replaced with a new, sturdy substructure and hardwood decking boards. The old, dated iron railing was replaced with a custom-built wooden slatted screen, which a climbing plant now grows up for extra privacy and greenery. The result is a balcony transformed from a neglected corner into a proper outdoor space. Renovating a balcony or replacing a rotten balcony floor in Haarlem? Handymat Services handles it from demolition through to the final finish.

Staircase renovation | Haarlem

This staircase in Haarlem was part of a larger home renovation.
