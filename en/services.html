"What I can do for you"

From a small repair to a full bathroom or kitchen refresh. Don't see your job listed? Just get in touch — I'm always happy to think it through with you.

Bathroom

What I do: install a new washbasin, vanity unit or shower screen, repair or partially retile, redo the sealant, fix leaks, make small layout changes, replace fittings, and reroute water supply and drainage pipes.

What I don't do: anything involving the boiler or gas.

Kitchen

What I do: replace or install kitchen cabinets and worktops, redo the sealant, fix leaks, make small layout changes, connect appliances (non-gas), and finish off with skirting boards.

What I don't do: anything involving the boiler or gas.

Living room

What I do: lay laminate or other flooring, level floors, fit skirting boards, mount a TV, and hang curtain rails.

Painting & decorating

What I do: patch and touch up walls, interior painting, paint window frames and woodwork, and give walls a fresh coat.

Furniture

What I do: flatpack furniture won't assemble itself. I don't shy away from it — from assembling furniture to building wardrobes.

Lighting & technology

What I do: hang light fixtures, replace or relocate switches and sockets, install smoke alarms, set up TV/audio systems, boost wifi coverage with extra access points, and help with wifi and smart home setups.

What I don't do: work on the fuse box or meter cupboard.

Floors & windows

What I do: replace HR++ (high-performance double) glazing in individual, easily accessible windows, lay flooring, adjust doors or windows, and fit skirting boards.

What I don't do: reglaze entire properties where scaffolding would be needed.

Outdoors

What I do: lay a garden path, repair a fence, and spruce up a garden shed.

Specialities

Landlords & property management

A single point of contact for turnover maintenance, repairs and handovers of rental properties.

IT services

Wifi, smart home, laptop repairs, and hardware or software issues on Mac and Windows — solved with 28 years of IT experience.

Wall panel installation

Freshen up a wall with panels fitted straight over existing tiles or plaster, no demolition needed. Great for bathrooms, hallways and other spaces.
